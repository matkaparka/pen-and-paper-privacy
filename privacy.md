# Privacy policy · 隐私说明

Pen & Paper (纸笔) for Android, package `io.github.matkaparka.zhibi`. Last updated 2026-10-16.

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

Pen & Paper does not collect, store, send or share any personal data.

- The app has no internet access (it does not request the Internet permission), no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
- Your drawings stay on your device. The app only reads a file when you pick it in the system file picker, and only writes a file where you choose to save or export it. While it works, it keeps temporary copies in its own private storage on your device; uninstalling the app removes them.
- The app's settings (tool zones, swatches and the like) are stored on your device only.

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the change ships. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/matkaparka/pen-and-paper-privacy/issues.

## 中文

纸笔不收集、不保存、不发送、不分享任何个人数据。

- 程序不联网（不申请网络权限），没有账号、广告、统计和崩溃上报。
- 你的画只在你的设备上。程序只在你用系统的文件选择器选了文件时读那个文件，只把文件写到你选的保存、导出位置。工作时会在程序自己的私有目录里放临时的副本，卸载程序时一起删掉。
- 程序的设置（工具分区、常用色等）只存在你的设备上。

以后如果有变化，会先在这一页写明。有问题请在 https://github.com/matkaparka/pen-and-paper-privacy/issues 提出。
